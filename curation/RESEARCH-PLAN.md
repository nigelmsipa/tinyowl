# Tiny Owl — ILK Research Plan

## Overview

230 questions from the "I'd Like to Know" series are organized below into 15 thematic research groups.

### Workflow

1. Take the research prompt for a group to your deep research tool (Perplexity Deep Research, Gemini Deep Research, NotebookLM with relevant sources, etc.)
2. The prompt names every question explicitly — the research output must address each one directly, not just treat the theme generally
3. Research output becomes the primary bolstering layer for each Q&A entry
4. The speaker's transcript provides the human anchor (their words, their framing, their argument)
5. Personal editorial input added as needed

### Answer construction per entry (three layers)

```
Layer 1 — Human anchor:   what the speaker actually said and argued
Layer 2 — Research layer:  scripture, historical context, theological scholarship
Layer 3 — Editorial input: personal clarifications, corrections, or additions
```

### Research prompt structure

Each group's prompt is formatted for copy-paste use. It:
- States the research task clearly
- Lists every question by ILK ID and title
- Requires a dedicated response per question with: primary scripture basis, theological argument, historical context where applicable, and a direct answer
- Instructs that scripture be quoted or paraphrased with references cited in the prose, not treated as decoration

> You are producing a dedicated scripture-grounded answer for each of the following questions. > - **ilk-177** — What does Colossians 2:16–17 mean by "let no one judge you in respect of a sabbath"? Is Paul abolishing Sabbath observance or defending believers against legalistic judgment?
> - **ilk-178** — Did the Sabbath exist before Creation Week? What is its cosmic and pre-Sinai basis?
> - **ilk-200** — What did Paul mean in Ephesians 2:14–16 when Christ abolished "the law of commandments contained in ordinances"? Which law is in view — the moral law, the ceremonial law, or the Mosaic civil code?
