# Answering Atheism — Spirit of Prophecy Prompts

For each question cluster in `RESEARCH-PLAN.md`, these prompts extract the relevant Ellen White material. Run these against the SOP corpus (NotebookLM or direct search) to build the SOP layer for each answer.

---

## Cluster A — Historical and Evidential

**SOP prompt for aa-001 (500 witnesses / resurrection reliability):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Her treatment of the resurrection appearances — who saw the risen Christ, in what order, and what she says about the certainty of those encounters. Key source: *The Desire of Ages*, chapters 81–82 ("The Lord Is Risen," "Why Weepest Thou").
> 2. Any statements she makes about the historical reliability of the Gospel accounts or the apostolic witness.
> 3. Does she address the sufficiency of witness testimony for faith? How does she frame the evidence for the resurrection versus the faith required to accept it?

**SOP prompt for aa-002 (flood / geological evidence):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Her description of the pre-flood world and the physical changes wrought by the flood. Key source: *Patriarchs and Prophets*, chapters 7–8 ("The Flood" and surrounding chapters).
> 2. Any statements she makes about what physical evidence of the flood looks like — buried organisms, geological strata, coal and fossil deposits. She makes specific claims about this.
> 3. Her framing of the flood as a literal, global event and why this matters theologically.
> 4. Any statements about creation science or the relationship between Scripture and geology.

**SOP prompt for aa-003 (overstated Christian claims / distinguishing Scripture from what Christians say):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Warnings against building faith on uncertain foundations or popular apologetics claims that cannot be sustained.
> 2. Her distinction between the authority of Scripture and the authority of human interpretation or tradition.
> 3. Statements about intellectual honesty in faith — is doubt in response to evidence a sin, or a call to deeper investigation?
> 4. *Steps to Christ* chapter "What to Do with Doubt" — her approach to honest intellectual struggle.

## Cluster B — Doctrinal Corrections

**SOP prompt for aa-004 (eternal torment / state of the dead):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Her explicit rejection of eternal torment as a false doctrine. Key source: *The Great Controversy*, chapters "Spiritualism" and "Can Our Dead Speak to Us?"
> 2. Her argument that the doctrine of eternal conscious torment misrepresents God's character and drives people away from Him — this is directly relevant to Promise Backland's experience.
> 3. Her description of what actually happens at death: unconscious sleep, no awareness, no communication with the living.
> 4. Any statement linking the eternal-torment doctrine to Satan's deception strategy — the idea that this false doctrine is one of the enemy's tools to make God appear monstrous.

**SOP prompt for aa-005 (false doctrine contributed to deconversion):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Statements about how false views of God's character and false doctrines drive souls away from Him. She writes about this explicitly — the God portrayed in eternal torment theology is not the God of Scripture, and this misrepresentation has consequences.
> 2. Any statements about people who rejected Christianity and whether their rejection was of the true gospel or of a corrupted form of it.
> 3. Her warnings to the church about the damage done when false doctrine is taught as if it were Scripture.

## Cluster C — Faith, Evidence, and the Nature of Belief

**SOP prompt for aa-006 (rational belief / good reason to believe):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Her treatment of faith and evidence — specifically, does she teach blind faith or evidenced faith? *Steps to Christ* is the primary source.
> 2. Her statement (paraphrased widely): "God never asks us to believe without giving sufficient evidence upon which to base our faith." Locate the exact quotation and context.
> 3. Romans 1:20 and the argument from nature — does she develop this? *Education* and *Patriarchs and Prophets* on creation as evidence.
> 4. The distinction between faith and presumption in her writings.

**SOP prompt for aa-007 (genuine faith and deconversion / "you never really believed"):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Her treatment of the parable of the sower — especially the seed on rocky ground (Matthew 13:20-21). What does she say about people whose faith appeared genuine but did not endure?
> 2. Any statements about the danger of dismissing or judging the sincerity of those who have left the faith.
> 3. Her descriptions of what genuine, saving faith actually consists of — how does it differ from intellectual assent alone?
> 4. Statements about the role of the Holy Spirit in sustaining faith — and what happens when a person is cut off from that connection.

**SOP prompt for aa-008 (unanswered prayer):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Her chapter on prayer in *Steps to Christ* — what she says about God's responses, timing, and apparent silence.
> 2. Any statements about what God's silence in prayer means — does silence indicate absence?
> 3. The conditions she identifies for effective prayer — not as mechanical requirements, but as descriptions of the posture in which prayer is heard.
> 4. Gethsemane as the model of prayer that was not answered in the way it was asked — how does she treat this?

## Cluster D — The Church, Hypocrisy, and Politics

**SOP prompt for aa-009 (leadership failure / hypocrisy / faith in spite of bad leaders):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Her warnings about leaders in the church losing their spiritual bearings — particularly regarding political alliances and worldly power. *Testimonies to the Church* and *The Great Controversy* are the primary sources.
> 2. Her statement (from memory, locate exact): that we should not make flesh our arm — that dependence on human leaders is a spiritual danger.
> 3. Ezekiel 34 parallels — does she invoke the "false shepherd" language?
> 4. Her counsel to members who are disillusioned by leadership failure: what does she tell them to do? Does she tell them to stay or leave?
> 5. Any statements about the difference between the institution and the Lord of the institution — the church can fail; Christ does not.

**SOP prompt for aa-010 (religious nationalism / Revelation 13 / church-state fusion):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. *The Great Controversy* chapters: "The Impending Conflict," "Liberty of Conscience Threatened," "The Final Warning," "The Time of Trouble." Her predictions about the fusion of religious and political power in the United States.
> 2. Her specific description of what the "image of the beast" looks like when it forms — she describes it as a church-state union that enforces religious observance by civil law.
> 3. Any statements about religious nationalism as a prophetic sign — that the blending of patriotism and religion is itself a warning signal.
> 4. Her counsel to Adventists about how to relate to civil government and political movements without being swept up in them.

## Cluster E — Morality and Meaning Without God

**SOP prompt for aa-011 (moral improvement after leaving Christianity):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Her treatment of morality that is not rooted in love for God — what does she say about "natural" virtue or moral improvement achieved by human effort?
> 2. The distinction she draws between outward moral conformity and genuine character transformation. *Steps to Christ* and *Thoughts from the Mount of Blessing* are the primary sources.
> 3. Any statements about people who appear to improve morally after leaving Christianity — does she address this possibility?
> 4. Her argument that genuine love for others is the fruit of the Spirit, not merely the result of good intentions — and what this means for evaluating self-reported moral improvement.

**SOP prompt for aa-012 (does the atheist secretly know God exists? / Romans 1):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Her treatment of Romans 1:20 — the light available to everyone through creation.
> 2. Any statements about sincere atheists or people who genuinely cannot find God — does she say they are suppressing truth, or does she allow for genuine honest doubt?
> 3. Her treatment of conscience as a remnant of the image of God — even in people who do not believe, is the moral sense operative?
> 4. *Education* on the knowledge of God available through nature.

## Cluster F — Religious Trauma

**SOP prompt for aa-013 (religious trauma / OCD / depression / mental health):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Her statements on the relationship between mental, physical, and spiritual health — she is explicit that these are connected. *The Ministry of Healing* is the primary source.
> 2. Any statements about depression, anxiety, or mental suffering in a religious context — does she treat these as purely spiritual problems ("attacks of the enemy") or does she acknowledge physical and psychological causes?
> 3. Her counsel on the use of medical care, therapy, and rest for mental suffering — she was not a faith-healer who rejected medicine.
> 4. Any statements about harm done by religious environments — guilt, fear, compulsion — that damage rather than heal.
> 5. Her description of the character of God as a God of love, not fear — and how false presentations of God create emotional damage.

## Cluster G — Jesus-Specific Objections

**SOP prompt for aa-014 (the sacrifice / day and a half):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Her description of Gethsemane and Calvary in *The Desire of Ages* (chapters 74–80). She gives the richest available description of what Christ actually experienced — not primarily the physical suffering, but the weight of human sin and the separation from the Father.
> 2. Her specific treatment of Matthew 27:46 — "My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?" What does she say was happening at that moment? Does she describe it as the second death?
> 3. Her statements about why Christ's death was uniquely sufficient — what made it different from every other death in history?
> 4. Any statement about the resurrection's relationship to the sacrifice — does the resurrection undo or vindicate the cross?
> 5. *Desire of Ages* chapter "It Is Finished" — her treatment of the completeness of the atonement at the moment of Christ's death.

**SOP prompt for aa-015 (bad communicator / parables / contradictions):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. *Christ's Object Lessons* — the entire book is her treatment of why Jesus used parables and what they reveal. Her introduction is particularly relevant to the "bad communicator" objection.
> 2. Her statement on why Jesus spoke in parables — not to hide truth from sincere seekers, but to protect truth from those who would only misuse it; and to make truth memorable across generations.
> 3. Any statements about the unity of Christ's teaching — does she address apparent contradictions in his words?
> 4. Her treatment of the Sermon on the Mount in *Thoughts from the Mount of Blessing* — particularly the sections on non-retaliation and love of enemies, which bear on the sword/cheek objection.

**SOP prompt for aa-016 (Jesus and slavery):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Any statements she makes about slavery as an institution — she wrote during and after the American Civil War and addressed it directly.
> 2. Her treatment of Galatians 3:28 or Philemon — the passages that embed the seeds of abolition in Christian theology.
> 3. Her statements about the dignity of every human being regardless of race or social status — and how this flows from the gospel.
> 4. Any statements about the relationship between the Mosaic civil law and the moral law — does she distinguish between laws that regulate fallen social structures and laws that express God's character?

**SOP prompt for aa-017 (cult-like demands / hate your family):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Her treatment of Luke 14:26 ("hate your father and mother") — does she address this directly? What does she say the demand means?
> 2. Her statements about family loyalty vs. loyalty to God — what is the order of priorities?
> 3. Any treatment of the cost of discipleship — does she acknowledge that following truth sometimes means separation from family, and how does she frame that cost?
> 4. Her statements about what the gospel does to families — does it destroy them or redeem them?

**SOP prompt for aa-018 (Golden Rule oversimplified):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Her treatment of Matthew 7:12 — does she develop the Golden Rule, and how does she frame its scope?
> 2. *Thoughts from the Mount of Blessing* — her commentary on the Sermon on the Mount, including the Golden Rule section.
> 3. Her statements about the law of love as the foundation of all ethics — does she present the Golden Rule as a principle or as a specific behavioral rule?
> 4. Any statements about the difference between the spirit of the law and a mechanical application of it.

**SOP prompt for aa-019 (limited ministry / selective miracles):**
> Search Ellen White's writings for:
> 1. Her description of the scope of Christ's ministry in *The Desire of Ages* — what does she say about what he accomplished in three years?
> 2. Any statements about why Christ healed some and not others — does she address the selectivity of miracles?
> 3. Her treatment of John 21:25 ("there are also many other things that Jesus did") — the incompleteness of the Gospel record.
> 4. Her comparison of Christ to other religious teachers or philosophers — does she engage the question of his uniqueness among teachers?
> 5. Her framing of Christ's ministry as the greatest event in cosmic history — not measured by scope of miracles but by the nature of what was accomplished.
