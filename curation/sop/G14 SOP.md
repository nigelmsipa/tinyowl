**Drums and Noise in Worship (ilk-011)**
Drums and loud, boisterous noise are not acceptable in Christian worship. The sources describe the use of a "bedlam of noise" with drums, music, and dancing as an "invention of Satan" designed to shock the senses, confuse the mind, and cover up his deceptive methods (*A New Life*, "Beware the Counterfeits"). God's work is to be characterized by calmness, dignity, and godly fear, not a carnival of noise or irrational behavior (*Selected Messages Book 2*, "The 'Holy Flesh' Doctrine").

**Women in Ministry and Ordination (ilk-031, ilk-226)**
Women are highly encouraged to participate in ministry, preach the Word, and give Bible readings to congregations (*Daughters of God*, "The Labourer Is Worthy of His Hire"). The sources note that women can reach classes of people that men cannot, and they should be paid wages proportionate to ordained ministers (*Daughters of God*, "The Lord Calls Women to His Service").
However, regarding formal ordination as ministers, the sources state there is no explicit encouragement from the author's pen for "the formal and official ordination of women to the gospel ministry" (*Daughters of God*, "Appendix C—Exhibits Relating to the Ordination of Women"). She did, however, strongly recommend that women be set apart for visiting the sick and poor by "prayer and laying on of hands," which is a deaconess-type work (*Pastoral Ministry*, "Women as Soulwinners").

**Cross-dressing and Women in Pants (ilk-032, ilk-038)**
Cross-dressing and women wearing men's clothing are described as a sin and an abomination. God designed a plain distinction between the dress of men and women, and reversing this order causes confusion and an increase in crime (*Testimonies for the Church, vol. 1*, "Reform in Dress"). While a "reform dress" advocating for pants (the "American costume") was introduced during this era, it was explicitly condemned in the texts as immodest and wholly unfitted for the followers of Christ, with warnings that adopting such styles would destroy the church's influence (*Testimonies for the Church, vol. 1*, "Reform in Dress").

**Dealing with Hypocrites and Disregard for Principles (ilk-041, ilk-055)**
When dealing with difficult or erring members, believers must strictly follow Christ's rule in Matthew 18: go to the person alone in a spirit of meekness and love, rather than gossiping or exposing their faults to others (*Counsels for the Church*, "Treatment of the Erring"). If they do not listen, take one or two others.
If a member persistently disregards clear biblical principles and refuses all efforts by the church to reclaim them, the church has the solemn responsibility to separate them from fellowship and remove their names from the books (*Testimonies for the Church, vol. 5*, "Church Duties"). Heaven will ratify the decision made by the church in cutting off the unrepentant offender (*Testimonies for the Church, vol. 5*, "Church Duties").

**Getting Involved in Ministry (ilk-089)**
Every Christian is born into the kingdom of God as a missionary. You can get involved by taking up the duties lying directly in your pathway, rather than waiting for a grand public role (*Christian Service*, "God's Call to Service"). Take hold wherever you see that there is work to be done. This includes engaging in personal evangelism, distributing tracts, giving Bible readings, and living out your faith faithfully in your secular business or your family (*Testimonies for the Church, vol. 6*, "The Home Missionary Work").

**Honoring Unbelieving Parents (ilk-090)**
The command to honor your father and mother is binding upon children of all ages, even if the parents are unbelieving or disagreeable (*The Adventist Home*, "The Honor Due Parents"). The only exception is if an unbelieving parent's commands directly contradict the requirements of Christ; in that case, the child must obey God and trust the consequences to Him.

**Is God Against Polygamy? (ilk-104)**
Yes. The texts state that God "has not sanctioned polygamy in a single instance." It is entirely contrary to His will, and He knew that the happiness of humanity would be destroyed by it (*Spiritual Gifts, vol. 3*, "Abraham").

**Must Christians Marry? (ilk-133)**
Christians are not required to marry, but celibacy is not enforced either. Jesus came not to destroy the sacred relationship of marriage, but to exalt it and restore it to its original sanctity (*The Adventist Home*, "Marital Duties and Privileges"). However, the sources suggest that remaining single (like the Apostle Paul) is a commendable course for those who wish to be wholly the Lord's while waiting for His coming, unless marrying undeniably betters their condition (*Testimonies on Sexual Behavior, Adultery, and Divorce*, "An Early Church Problem").

**What a Christian Should Do During a Divorce (ilk-206)**
The Bible gives only one valid reason for divorce, which is adultery (*Testimonies on Sexual Behavior, Adultery, and Divorce*, "Grounds for Divorce"). A woman or man may be legally divorced by the laws of the land for other reasons, but they are not divorced in the sight of God, and marrying another while the spouse lives violates the seventh commandment (*Testimonies on Sexual Behavior, Adultery, and Divorce*, "Grounds for Divorce"). If a spouse is an unbeliever and an opposer, the Christian must abide with them patiently, living a Christlike life, unless the unbelieving spouse chooses of their own free will to depart (*Testimonies on Sexual Behavior, Adultery, and Divorce*, "The Christian Spouse's Behavior").

**Hats and Jewelry in Church (ilk-198, ilk-222)**
Wearing a neat, plain hat (like a straw or silk bonnet) in church is not a sin, as long as it avoids extremes and ostentatious displays (*Spiritual Gifts, vol. 4b*, "Great Distress Coming, and God's People Not Prepared For It").
However, wearing jewelry, gold, pearls, or engaging in the "idolatry of dress" is a serious salvational issue. Extravagant dress and jewelry foster pride, stifle the desire to do good, and represent a moral disease (*Evangelism*, "Presenting Health and Christian Standards"). The sources warn that those who cling to these forbidden ornaments and refuse to repent cherish vanity in the heart and will not enter the new earth (*Testimonies for the Church, vol. 4*, "Simplicity in Dress").

**Prioritizing Work Over Parenting (ilk-227)**
It is entirely wrong for parents to prioritize work over parenting. In no case are parents to let business, or even supposed religious duties and public ministry, absorb their time and talents to the neglect of their children (*Gospel Workers*, "The Minister in His Home"). Great good done for others cannot cancel the debt that a parent owes to God to care for their own children (*Gospel Workers*, "The Minister in His Home"). If a mother or father ignores their home duties to engage in another work they suppose is holier, they are under a deception (*The Adventist Home*, "Misconception of the Mother's Work").
