**666 and the United States in End-Time Prophecy (ilk-002)**
In Bible prophecy, the beast seen "coming up out of the earth" with two horns like a lamb (Revelation 13:11) unmistakably points to the United States of America (*The Great Controversy*, "God's Law Immutable"). It arose peacefully in a previously unoccupied territory around 1798, and its lamb-like horns represent the nation's foundational principles of civil and religious liberty—Republicanism and Protestantism (*The Great Controversy*, "God's Law Immutable"). However, the prophecy declares that the USA will eventually "speak as a dragon" by contradicting its own free institutions (*The Great Controversy*, "God's Law Immutable"). It will form an "image to the beast" by enforcing a religious observance—Sunday worship—using the power of the state, thereby paying homage to the Papacy (*The Great Controversy*, "God's Law Immutable"). Regarding "666," the sources briefly mention that "the number (666) of the Image Beast was made up" and is connected to the beast that changed the Sabbath to Sunday (*A Word to the Little Flock*, "To Bro. Eli Curtis").

**Buying and Selling in Revelation 13:17 (ilk-026)**
The prohibition against "buying and selling" is described as a literal economic exclusion. As the final conflict escalates, those who remain loyal to God's law and refuse to observe the false sabbath will face civil penalties and a strict boycott; they "will be forbidden to buy or sell" to pressure them into compliance, cutting off every earthly support (*Historical Sketches of Foreign Missions*, "Satan An Accuser of the Brethren"; *The Great Controversy*, "The Impending Conflict").

**The Seven Heads and France's Role (ilk-077)**
The provided texts do not explicitly define what the seven heads of Revelation 17 represent. They do, however, detail the prophetic role of France. During the French Revolution, the nation manifested the characteristics of "the beast that ascendeth out of the bottomless pit" (Revelation 11), making open, atheistic war upon the Bible and the "two witnesses," mirroring the spiritual corruption of Sodom and Egypt (*The Great Controversy*, "The Bible and the French Revolution"). Furthermore, in 1798, the French army took the pope captive, inflicting the "deadly wound" on the Papacy and ending its 1260 years of oppressive supremacy (*The Great Controversy*, "The Bible and the French Revolution"; *The Great Controversy*, "God's Law Immutable").

**The Remnant Church (ilk-135)**
The sources do not prophesy the creation of a brand new, separate church in the last days. Instead, God's existing remnant church is identified as those "which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ" (*Testimonies to Ministers and Gospel Workers*, "The Church of Christ"). Though this church is described as currently "enfeebled and defective," it remains "the only object upon earth upon which Christ bestows His supreme regard" (*Testimonies to Ministers and Gospel Workers*, "The Church of Christ"). God's plan is to purify this existing body, and during the final "loud cry," He will call His faithful people out of fallen Babylon (apostate churches) to join this commandment-keeping remnant (*The Great Controversy*, "The Final Warning"; *Early Writings*, "The Loud Cry").

**The Angels of Revelation 7 and 16 (ilk-148)**
The angels of Revelation 7 (holding the four winds) and Revelation 16 (pouring out the plagues) represent literal heavenly angels. The texts state that "four mighty angels hold back the powers of this earth" to restrain global conflict (*Testimonies to Ministers and Gospel Workers*, "The Message of Revelation 14"; *Early Writings*, "The Sealing"). Similarly, the plagues will be poured out by literal angels exercising destructive power, just as "a single angel destroyed all the first-born of the Egyptians" (*The Great Controversy*, "The Time of Trouble"). Conversely, the three angels of Revelation 14 are symbolic, representing human messengers who proclaim God's warning to the world (*Life Sketches of Ellen G. White*, "The Great Controversy").

**Deception of the Elect and Matthew 24:24 (ilk-169)**
In the final crisis, Satan will appear as an angel of light, personate Christ, and perform genuine miracles—including bringing fire down from heaven—to deceive the world (*The Great Controversy*, "The Time of Trouble"; *Evangelism*, "Maintaining a High Moral Standard"). He will quote Scripture and command Sunday worship (*The Great Controversy*, "The Time of Trouble"). However, the elect will not be deceived. They will recognize that Satan's teachings contradict the Bible, and they will find their safety not in miracles, but by taking their stand solely on the living Word of God ("It is written") and the Sabbath commandment (*The Great Controversy*, "The Time of Trouble"; *The Great Controversy*, "The Scriptures a Safeguard").

**The Close of Probation (ilk-171)**
At the close of probation, the third angel's message finishes, the sealing of God's people is complete, and Christ ceases His intercessory work in the heavenly sanctuary (*The Great Controversy*, "The Time of Trouble"; *The Story of Redemption*, "The Close of Probation"). He declares, "It is done," and the eternal destiny of every soul is fixed (*The Great Controversy*, "The Time of Trouble"; *The Story of Redemption*, "The Close of Probation"). The restraining Spirit of God is entirely withdrawn from the wicked, leaving Satan in full control of the impenitent (*The Great Controversy*, "The Time of Trouble"). While the unmingled wrath of God (the seven last plagues) begins to fall (*Early Writings*, "The Time of Jacob's Trouble"), the righteous must live in the sight of a holy God without an Intercessor during the "time of Jacob's trouble" (*The Great Controversy*, "The Time of Trouble"). The people of the world will be utterly unaware that their probation has ended, continuing their daily routines as in the days of Noah (*The Great Controversy*, "The Time of Trouble"; *The Story of Redemption*, "The Close of Probation").

**The 144,000 and the Great Multitude (ilk-173)**

* **The 144,000** are a specific group of living saints who are translated at the Second Coming without seeing death (*The Great Controversy*, "God's People Delivered"). They sing a "new song" that no one else can learn because they passed through the final, unparalleled time of trouble without an Intercessor and were the "firstfruits unto God" (*The Great Controversy*, "God's People Delivered"; *Acts of the Apostles*, "A Seeker for Truth").
* **The Great Multitude** (Revelation 7:9) is comprised of the redeemed from all ages. This includes those who perfected Christian character amidst infidelity, the millions who were martyred for their faith, and those from every nation, kindred, and tongue who have washed their robes in the blood of the Lamb (*The Great Controversy*, "God's People Delivered"; *Testimonies for the Church, vol. 9*, "The Reward of Earnest Effort").

**The Biblical Second Coming vs. The Rapture (ilk-176)**
Please note that the sources do not explicitly use the term "rapture teaching," but they clearly define the nature of the Second Coming. The biblical Second Coming is described as a highly visible, catastrophic, and glorious event, not a secret disappearance. Christ descends upon a cloud wrapped in flaming fire, accompanied by all the heavenly angels (*The Great Controversy*, "God's People Delivered"; *Early Writings*, "Deliverance of the Saints"). The heavens roll together as a scroll, massive earthquakes strike, and the wicked, unable to stand His glory, cry out for the rocks to fall on them before being destroyed by the brightness of His coming (*The Great Controversy*, "God's People Delivered"; *The Great Hope*, "Victory of Love"). Simultaneously, the righteous dead are resurrected and, together with the transformed living saints, are caught up to meet the Lord (*The Great Controversy*, "God's People Delivered").
