**How to Study the Bible (ilk-094)**
A Christian should study the Bible daily with a humble, prayerful heart, seeking to know God's revealed will rather than trying to prove a preconceived theory (*Special Testimonies On Education*, "True Education"). Instead of relying on human wisdom or the opinions of learned men, the student must compare "scripture with scripture" to find the true weight of evidence (*Counsels on Sabbath School Work*, "A School for Bible Study"). We are to accept the Bible as the unerring guide and the literal voice of God.

**Does the Bible Contradict Itself? (ilk-170)**
No. The teachings of God's Word on important points are "neither obscure nor contradictory; they may be understood by common minds" (*The Great Controversy*, "The First Great Deception"). While skeptics claim to find contradictions between science and revelation, the sources affirm that "rightly understood, they are in perfect harmony" (*Patriarchs and Prophets*, "The Literal Week").

**Evolution and the Age of the Earth (ilk-071, ilk-092)**
There is no evidence for evolution in the Bible; in fact, the sources strongly condemn it. The theory that attributes the origin of man to an "ascending line of germs, mollusks, and quadrupeds" is rejected as false science (*Education*, "Science and the Bible"). The assumption that creation took thousands or millions of years is labeled "infidelity in its most insidious... form" (*Patriarchs and Prophets*, "The Literal Week"). The Bible teaches that God created the world in six literal, 24-hour days, marked off by the rising and setting of the sun (*Testimonies to Ministers and Gospel Workers*, "God's High Standard").

**The Flat Earth (ilk-075)**
The shape of the earth is not a salvational issue or a point of debate in the texts. They simply accept a spherical earth, noting that "God made His Sabbath for a round world; and when the seventh day comes to us in that round world, controlled by the sun that rules the day, it is the time in all countries and lands to observe the Sabbath" (*Selected Messages Book 3*, "The Question of the Date Line").

**Black Slavery (ilk-021)**
The religion of the Bible "recognizes no caste or color" (*Testimonies for the Church, vol. 9*, "Consideration for Colored Laborers"). God's eighth commandment ("Thou shalt not steal") explicitly "condemns manstealing and slave dealing" (*From Eternity Past*, "The Law of God"). The sources state that the Lord looked with sadness upon the "colored race in slavery," noting they had been "educated in crime, degraded, and oppressed," and asserts their God-given "right to the blessings of freedom" (*Selected Messages Book 2*, "The Brotherhood of Mankind").

**Poverty (ilk-035)**
Christianity does not keep people in poverty. In God's original plan for Israel, every family was provided a home on the land to encourage an industrious, self-supporting life, preventing "extremes of wealth or poverty" (*From Eternity Past*, "God's Care for the Economically Disadvantaged"). Furthermore, God's Word "sanctions no policy that will enrich one class by the oppression and suffering of another" (*A Call to Stand Apart*, "Social Justice").

**Abuse of Women (ilk-007)**
While the sources do not explicitly discuss whether the Old Testament condones abuse, they strongly condemn men who mistreat women. They state that lustful men whose passions force women to lives of "hardship, pain, and suffering" could more rightly "be called brutes" than husbands (*Counsels on Health*, "Relation of Diet to Health and Morals"; *Testimonies on Sexual Behavior, Adultery, and Divorce*, "Important Facts and Principles").

**Competitive Sports (ilk-036)**
While modern competitive sports are not directly named, the sources contrast "Manual Labor Versus Games," indicating that God's plan involves "contented industry" and physical labor rather than spending time on idle games or frivolous amusements (*Counsels to Parents, Teachers, and Students*, "Study And Labor").

**Thou Shalt Not Kill vs. Capital Punishment and War (ilk-183)**
The sixth commandment prohibits all acts of injustice that shorten life, as well as hatred, revenge, and "wars of conquest" (*From Eternity Past*, "The Law of God"). However, it does not universally prohibit God-ordained capital punishment or divine warfare. God Himself attached the death penalty to willful murder, stating that "Whoso sheddeth man's blood, by man shall his blood be shed" (*SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 1*, "Chapter 9"). Furthermore, God commanded Moses and the Israelites to wage war against wicked nations like the Midianites because they had beguiled Israel into transgressing God's commandments (*Selected Messages Book 2*, "On the Military Question").

**Sacrificial Killing (ilk-152)**
God commanded the sacrifice of animals to typify the coming sacrifice of Christ (*From Eternity Past*, "Satan and Man at War"). However, He absolutely condemned the human sacrifices practiced by heathen nations (such as passing children through the fire), classifying these as satanic cruelty and a violation of the sixth commandment (*From Eternity Past*, "Satan and Man at War").

**Killing Insects (ilk-196)**
Killing annoying or destructive insects is not sinful. The idea that "nothing that has life should be killed, not even insects" is rebuked as a false, fanatical teaching (*Selected Messages Book 1*, "Fanciful or Speculative Teachings"). God gave no such message; believers are told to destroy pests to preserve themselves and their possessions from harm (*Selected Messages Book 3*, "Counsels on Many Matters").

**Abel's Blood Crying Out (ilk-023)**
The phrase "the blood cries out" is used to describe the guilt of taking a life or leading someone else into sin. Just as the voice of Abel's blood cried to God from the ground against Cain, the ruin of any victim led astray will cry unto God against the one who corrupted them (*SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 1*, "Chapter 4").

**Reaching the Unreached (ilk-147)**
Christians have a solemn obligation to reach the "millions upon millions of human souls ready to perish" who have never heard of Christ's love (*The Great Hope*, "In Defense of the Truth"). However, God's judgment is merciful toward those who die in ignorance. His test for the heathen is different than for those with great light: "He accepts from those in heathen lands a phase of righteousness which does not satisfy Him when offered by those of Christian lands. He does not require much where much has not been bestowed" (*SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 5*, "Chapter 12").

**Adam Made in the Image of God (ilk-095)**
Adam was created as a "counterpart of God" (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Flesh Meats"). Being made in God's image meant that "his nature was in harmony with the will of God, his mind capable of comprehending divine things. His affections were pure; his appetites and passions were under the control of reason" (*From Eternity Past*, "Creation: God's Answer to Evolution").

**Shouting While Sharing the Message (ilk-158)**
While "shouting" is not extensively detailed, the sources emphasize that God's work should be "quiet, calm, unpretending" (*Selected Messages Book 2*, "The Divine Credentials"). Effective speaking requires bringing home truths with simple language rather than producing an "ecstasy of feeling" or sensationalism (*Selected Messages Book 2*, "The Divine Credentials").

**Who or What is the Bride of the Lamb? (ilk-204)**
"The church is the bride of Christ, and her members are to yoke up with their Leader" (*SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 7*, "Chapter 18").
