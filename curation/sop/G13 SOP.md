**Coffee: Medically, Spiritually, and as a Sin (ilk-037, ilk-101)**
The sources categorize the drinking of tea and coffee as "a sin, an injurious indulgence, which, like other evils, injures the soul" (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Tea and Coffee"). Medically, coffee is classified as a "hurtful indulgence" and a "slow poison" (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Tea and Coffee"). It acts as a stimulant that temporarily exhilarates the nerves but leaves the system exhausted, leading to "prostration, paralysis of the mental, moral, and physical powers" (*Temperance*, "Tea and Coffee").

Spiritually, coffee and tea benumb the brain and becloud the intellect, blunting the user's moral sensibilities so that "sin does not appear very sinful, and truth is not regarded of greater value than earthly treasure" (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Tea and Coffee"). Regarding salvation, the texts state that those who destroy their mental, moral, and physical health by indulgence "will lose heaven" (*Confrontation*, "Health and Happiness"). However, God is merciful to those who indulge in pernicious habits in ignorance; but once the matter is set before them in its true light, they are "guilty before God if they continue to indulge this gross appetite" (*Christian Temperance and Bible Hygiene*, "Christian Temperance General Principles").

**Meat in the Bible and the Clean Meat Question (ilk-057, ilk-065)**
The Bible does not universally forbid meat-eating. The original diet appointed by God in Eden consisted of fruits, grains, nuts, and vegetables, and God gave man no permission to eat animal food until after the Flood, when vegetation was destroyed (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Flesh Meats"). Regarding Jesus, the sources note that He miraculously fed the multitudes with "fishes and barley loaves that were the daily fare of the fisherfolk of Galilee" (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "An Adequate Diet").

However, the fact that clean meat and fish were eaten in biblical times does not mean they are optimal today. The sources declare that "if meat eating was ever healthful, it is not safe now" (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Flesh Meats"). Because of the increasing wickedness of man and the curse upon the earth, "animals are becoming more and more diseased" (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Health Foods and Hygienic Restaurants"). People are continually eating flesh filled with tuberculous and cancerous germs, which implants disease into the blood (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Flesh Meats"). Furthermore, a meat diet "animalizes the nature" and weakens spiritual perception, meaning that discarding it is a necessary step for God's remnant people (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Flesh Meats").

**The Practice of Fasting (ilk-103)**
Fasting is highly beneficial when practiced correctly, but dangerous when taken to extremes. Medically, intemperate eating is a frequent cause of sickness, and "the very best remedy is for the patient to fast for a meal or two, that the overworked organs of digestion may have an opportunity to rest" (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Fasting"). A short period of entire abstinence often brings great relief. Spiritually, true fasting involves abstaining from stimulating food and dedicating the mind to God (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Fasting"). However, prolonged fasting is physically and spiritually dangerous. The sources warn an individual who had fasted for two days: "God did not require it of you. I beg of you to be cautious and eat freely good, wholesome food twice a day. You will surely decrease in strength and your mind become unbalanced" (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Fasting").

**Salvation, Mission, and the Health Message (ilk-114)**
The health message is of paramount importance to both mission and salvation. The sources declare that health reform is "as closely connected with the third angel's message as the hand is with the body" (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Health Reform and the Third Angel's Message"). It is the Lord's chosen "entering wedge" to overcome prejudice, break down barriers, and open doors for the entrance of present truth (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Health Reform and the Third Angel's Message"). Regarding personal salvation, the body is the temple of the Holy Spirit, and "it is not possible for us to glorify God while living in violation of the laws of life" (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Diet and Spirituality"). Indulgence of appetite stupefies the conscience and makes the sanctification of the body and spirit impossible.

**Environmental Responsibility (ilk-203)**
While the texts do not extensively use modern terminology for environmentalism, they establish the Christian's responsibility to respect God's creation. God's original plan was for man to subsist on the products of the earth, and "He gave to Adam charge of the garden, to dress it, and to care for it" (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Flesh Meats"). The degradation of nature is tied directly to human sin; the text states that "disease is accumulating rapidly. The curse of God is upon the earth, because man has cursed it" (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Flesh Meats").

**Drinking Wine as a Salvational Issue (ilk-224)**
Drinking intoxicating wine is unequivocally a salvational issue. The Scriptures declare that "no drunkard shall inherit the kingdom of God" and that "there shall in no wise enter into it anything that defileth" (*Temperance*, "Defiling the Temple of God"). God has strictly forbidden the use of intoxicating beverages to save men from degradation and ruin. Even so-called "milder" drinks like cider and sweet wine are highly dangerous, as they undergo fermentation, awaken a taste for stronger stimulants, and lay the foundation of the drinking habit (*Temperance*, "The Intoxicating Effects of Wine and Cider"). The Christian standard regarding all alcoholic drinks is "touch not, taste not, handle not, tea, coffee, wines, tobacco, opium, and alcoholic drinks" (*Temperance*, "Total Abstinence Our Position").
