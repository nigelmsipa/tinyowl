**The Salvation of Infants, the Unaccountable, and Aborted Babies (ilk-005, ilk-019)**
The sources teach that the faith of believing parents covers their children, much like the blood on the doorposts in Egypt shielded the households of the Israelites (*Selected Messages Book 3*, "A Law of Nations That Will Cause Men to Violate God's Law"; *SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 1*, Comments on Genesis 1). At the resurrection, little infants will come forth immortal and "wing their way to their mothers' arms," meeting to part no more (*The Great Hope*, "Resurrection to Eternal Life"; *The Youth's Instructor*, "The Tree of Life"). Motherless infants are received by angels and conducted to the tree of life, where Jesus places crowns upon their heads (*The Youth's Instructor*, "The Tree of Life"; *Last-Day Events*, "The Glad Reunion of Families").

Regarding those without the capacity to respond to God (such as imbeciles), the sources state that they are "without the consciousness of sin" and that the grace of God will remove their hereditary deficiencies, granting them an inheritance among the saints (*Welfare Ministry*, "Our Duty to the Unfortunate"). The sources state, "Whether all the children of unbelieving parents will be saved we cannot tell, because God has not made known His purpose in regard to this matter" (*Selected Messages Book 3*, "Many Children Will Be Taken Away").
*Note: The provided sources do not explicitly address the fate of aborted babies.*  
**The Sea of Glass (ilk-020)**
The sources do not mention a baptism occurring at the sea of glass. Instead, Revelation and the prophetic visions describe the redeemed ascending to the sea of glass, where Jesus personally places crowns on their heads and gives them harps of gold and palms of victory (*Early Writings*, "The Deliverance of the Saints"; *Christian Experience and Teachings of Ellen G. White*, "My First Vision"). Upon this sea of glass, the 144,000 stand in a perfect square, singing the song of Moses and the Lamb—a song of their unique experience and deliverance—before marching to the gates of the City (*Early Writings*, "The Deliverance of the Saints"; *Thoughts from the Mount of Blessing*, "The Beatitudes").  
**The Cosmic Scope of Creation and Earth's Significance (ilk-064, ilk-132)**
The Bible and nature reveal that God has "unnumbered worlds" inhabited by sinless intelligences who are obedient to His laws and who have watched the events on earth with intense interest (*Special Testimonies On Education*, "God in Nature"; *Patriarchs and Prophets*, "Why was Sin Permitted?"). The redeemed will be "unfettered by mortality" and will "wing their tireless flight to worlds afar," ranging from world to world to share in the knowledge gained by these unfallen beings (*The Great Controversy*, "The Controversy Ended"; *The Truth About Angels*, "The New Earth and Our Eternal Inheritance").

Our earth holds immense cosmic significance; it is called "the lesson book of the universe" (*From Heaven With Love*, "Christ Before Coming to Earth"). Though it is currently the "one dark blot in His glorious creation" due to the curse of sin, it will ultimately be "honored above all other worlds in the universe of God" (*The Desire of Ages*, "God With Us"). When Christ makes all things new, the earth—the very field Satan claimed as his—will be exalted to become the eternal headquarters where "the tabernacle of God shall be with men" (*The Desire of Ages*, "God With Us"; *The Story of Redemption*, "The New Jerusalem").  
**Free Choice and the Eradication of Sin (ilk-078)**
There will be free choice, but sin can never again enter the universe (*The Great Controversy*, "The Controversy Ended"). Satan's rebellion and the terrible results of sin serve as a "perpetual safeguard to all holy intelligences" (*SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 5*, Comments on John 8). Every character in heaven will have withstood the testing of evil and will no longer be susceptible to its power (*The Great Controversy*, "The Controversy Ended"). Because the nature and results of sin have been fully demonstrated, a "tested and proved creation will never again be turned from allegiance to Him whose character has been fully manifested before them as fathomless love and infinite wisdom" (*The Great Controversy*, "The Controversy Ended").  
**Procreation, Marriage, and Being "Like the Angels" (ilk-096, ilk-137, ilk-197)**
Jesus' words in Matthew 22:30 mean exactly what they say: there will be no marriages and no births in the new earth (*Medical Ministry*, "Speculation Regarding the Future Life"). The doctrine that children will be born in the new earth is not part of the "sure word of prophecy," and ideas about raising a family there "originate in the mind of the tempter" (*Medical Ministry*, "Speculation Regarding the Future Life"). Instead, the redeemed will be "as the angels of God, members of the royal family" (*Medical Ministry*, "Speculation Regarding the Future Life"). Being "like the angels" means they will partake of the pure, sinless character of angels, free from mortality, death, and earthly reproductive relationships (*Testimonies on Sexual Behavior, Adultery, and Divorce*, "Four Unholy Ministers").  
**Dietary Obedience and Salvation (ilk-141)**
The sources do not explicitly state that only vegetarians will be in heaven. However, they make it clear that dietary obedience and temperance heavily impact one's spiritual discernment and salvation. "Gluttons in heaven! No, no; such will never enter the pearly gates" (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Flesh Meats"). Those who gratify a gross appetite blunt their sensibilities, making it impossible to appreciate the exalted character of God (*Counsels on Diet and Foods*, "Flesh Meats"). Those who presume they can eat and drink as they please, ignoring health reform, will suffer the consequences and are engaging in the same sins of excess that doomed the antediluvian world and Sodom (*Temperance*, "Importance of Christ's Victory Over Appetite").  
**The Two Resurrections (ilk-156, ilk-160)**

* **The Special Resurrection:** Just prior to the Second Coming, during a mighty earthquake, a special resurrection occurs. Graves are opened, and "all who have died in the faith of the third angel's message" keeping the Sabbath come forth glorified to hear God's covenant of peace (*Early Writings*, "Deliverance of the Saints"). Additionally, those who "pierced Him" (mocked Christ's dying agonies) and the most violent opposers of His truth are raised at this time to behold His glory and the honor placed upon the obedient (*The Great Controversy*, "God's People Delivered").
* **The Second Resurrection:** At the close of the thousand years (the millennium), the second resurrection takes place (*The Story of Redemption*, "The Second Resurrection"). In this event, the wicked dead of all ages are raised to face the final execution of the judgment (*The Story of Redemption*, "The Second Resurrection"). They come up bearing the marks of the curse, disease, and death, standing in the same stature and with the same rebellious desires they had when they died (*The Great Controversy*, "The Controversy Ended").

**Memory of Earthly Lives in Heaven (ilk-221)**
We will indeed remember our earthly lives, but the pain and sorrow will be removed. The sources affirm that "we shall know even as also we are known," and every saint connected in family relationship here will recognize each other there (*Education*, "The School of the Hereafter"; *The Adventist Home*, "The Pen Pictures of the New Earth"). Furthermore, the redeemed will recognize and converse with those who led them to Christ, rejoicing together over their salvation (*Gospel Workers*, "The Reward of Earnest Effort"). While in heaven, all the perplexities of God's providence will be made plain; we will look back and see the perfect harmony and infinite love that ordered our most trying earthly experiences (*Testimonies for the Church, vol. 8*, "The Reward of Earnest Effort"). Yet, the pain of the past will not cast a shadow over eternity, for "former trials and suffering in this world will be forgotten and lost amid the glories of the New Earth" (*Selected Messages Book 3*, "The Reward of Earnest Effort").
