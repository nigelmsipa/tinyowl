Here is a comprehensive response to your questions regarding Satan, angels, and the origin of evil, based on the provided sources:

**Angelic Nature, Reproduction, and Record-Keeping (ilk-009, ilk-010, ilk-207)**
The sources do not mention "angelic heredity" or angels reproducing. As established in our previous conversation regarding the resurrection, angels are a distinct, sinless order of beings who do not marry or procreate (Matthew 22:30). While angels are literal beings created superior to humans (*The Truth About Angels*, "Angels Are Literal Beings"), the sources do not mention them carrying "golden cards." The scriptures and sources do, however, teach that "a faithful record is kept of the words and deeds of the children of men" by angel messengers (*The Great Controversy*, "The Investigative Judgment"). These recording angels chronicle "with terrible exactness" every wrong word, selfish act, and neglected duty opposite each person's name in the books of heaven (*The Great Controversy*, "The Investigative Judgment").  
**Fallen Angels vs. Demons (ilk-072)**
Fallen angels and demons are the exact same entities. The sources explicitly state that "evil spirits, in the beginning created sinless, were equal in nature, power, and glory with the holy beings... but fallen through sin, they are leagued together for the dishonor of God" (*The Great Controversy*, "Agency of Evil Spirits"). The Bible's references to "familiar spirits" or spirits of the dead are actually "evil angels, the messengers of Satan," and ancient idolatry was in reality "worshiping demons" (*Patriarchs and Prophets*, "Ancient and Modern Sorcery"; *The Great Controversy*, "Can Our Dead Speak to Us?").  
**Casting Out Demons Today (ilk-028)**
The gift of casting out demons is still operative for God's servants. The author explicitly confirms this regarding her own ministry: "In my labors in the cause of God, I have again and again met those who have been thus possessed, and in the name of the Lord I have rebuked the evil spirit" (*Selected Messages Book 2*, "Warnings Against Deceptive Claims of the Spirit's Guidance").  
**God, Lucifer, and the Origin of Evil (ilk-045, ilk-046, ilk-079, ilk-083)**
God did not create evil or Satan; He created a perfect, holy angel named Lucifer (*The Great Controversy*, "The Origin of Evil"). Sin is an unexplainable "intruder" for which no reason or excuse can be given, for to excuse it would be to defend it (*The Great Controversy*, "The Origin of Evil"). God created Lucifer as the "covering cherub, holy and undefiled," but gave him freedom of will because God "takes no pleasure in a forced allegiance" (*The Great Controversy*, "The Origin of Evil"). Little by little, Lucifer perverted this freedom, corrupted his wisdom, and indulged in a desire for self-exaltation and supremacy (*Patriarchs and Prophets*, "Why was Sin Permitted?"). Thus, God permitted evil by granting free will, but He did not originate it; evil is entirely the result of Lucifer's own choices (*The Great Controversy*, "The Origin of Evil").  
**Why God Did Not Prevent Sin (ilk-216)**
When Lucifer rebelled, God did not immediately destroy him because the universe was unprepared to comprehend the nature and consequences of sin (*Patriarchs and Prophets*, "Why was Sin Permitted?"). If God had blotted Satan out of existence instantly, the heavenly host "would have served God from fear rather than from love," and the spirit of rebellion would not have been fully eradicated (*Patriarchs and Prophets*, "Why was Sin Permitted?"). God permitted Satan time to fully develop his principles so that his charges against the divine government could be seen in their true, destructive light by all created beings (*Patriarchs and Prophets*, "Why was Sin Permitted?"; *The Great Controversy*, "The Origin of Evil").  
**Lucifer's Pre-Fall Rank and the Timing of the Fall (ilk-128, ilk-129, ilk-172)**
Before his fall, Lucifer was "next in honor to God's dear Son" and stood highest in power and glory among all the angels (*The Story of Redemption*, "The Fall of Lucifer"). His rebellion occurred before Creation Week was executed, but after God's purpose to create the earth was revealed. The Father had consulted Christ regarding the formation of man, and Lucifer became envious and jealous because he was not included in this secret counsel (*The Story of Redemption*, "The Fall of Lucifer"). War broke out in heaven, and Satan was expelled (*The Story of Redemption*, "The Fall of Lucifer"). When Adam and Eve were subsequently placed in the Garden of Eden, angels were sent to warn them about the "fallen foe" who was already plotting their ruin (*The Story of Redemption*, "Adam and Eve in Eden").  
**Satan's Access to Heaven (ilk-060)**
For a time after his fall, Satan still had access to present himself before God, as seen in Job 1-2, where he appeared among the angels "not to bow before the Eternal King, but to further his own malicious designs against the righteous" (*The Great Controversy*, "Agency of Evil Spirits"). However, this access changed at the cross. By shedding the blood of the Son of God, Satan was fully unmasked as a murderer, which "severed forever the last tie of sympathy" between him and the heavenly world (*The Desire of Ages*, "It Is Finished"). Christ's victory on Calvary accomplished "the casting down of Satan as an accuser of the brethren," and "henceforth he could no longer await the angels as they came from the heavenly courts" to accuse believers (*The Desire of Ages*, "It Is Finished").  
**Why Satan Engineered the Crucifixion (ilk-217)**
Satan engineered the crucifixion out of "deep-seated revenge" and intense hatred for Christ, who was a perfect reproach to sin (*The Desire of Ages*, "It Is Finished"). Satan hoped that by filling the Saviour's life with suffering, Christ would lose faith in His Father and fail to complete the atonement (*The Desire of Ages*, "Gethsemane"). If the plan of redemption failed, Satan believed he would retain permanent ownership of the earth and reign in opposition to God (*The Desire of Ages*, "It Is Finished"). However, his "hellish triumph" was short-lived; the crucifixion actually proved the immutability of God's law, sealed Satan's ultimate destruction, and forever uprooted him from the affections of the unfallen universe (*The Desire of Ages*, "It Is Finished").  
**The Holy Spirit and Angels (ilk-063)**
While the sources do not explicitly state that the Holy Spirit works through angels, they describe them as inseparable divine agencies cooperating for human salvation. Our sanctification is "the work of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit," and believers are called to "cooperate with the three great powers of heaven" (*Evangelism*, "The Message Triumphant"). Angels act as God's ministering spirits sent forth to guard and enlighten (*The Great Controversy*, "Agency of Evil Spirits"), while the Holy Spirit convicts and transforms the heart (*Steps to Christ*, "The Sinner's Need of Christ").  
**Satan's Genuine Miracles vs. Deceptions (ilk-155)**
Satan has the ability to perform actual miracles, not just sleight of hand. The sources affirm that "so far as his power extends, he will perform actual miracles" and "men are deceived by the miracles which Satan's agents have power to do, not which they pretend to do" (*The Great Controversy*, "The Time of Trouble"). However, his power has strict limits: he cannot create or give life (*Patriarchs and Prophets*, "The Plagues of Egypt"). For example, Pharaoh's magicians could not actually turn their rods into living serpents; they only produced a "counterfeit" appearance through demonic magic (*Patriarchs and Prophets*, "The Plagues of Egypt").  
**Satan Impersonating Jesus (ilk-154)**
As his crowning deception at the end of time, Satan will personate Jesus Christ, appearing as a majestic being of dazzling brightness, working mighty miracles, and claiming to be God (*The Great Controversy*, "The Time of Trouble"). However, the true Second Coming is distinguished from the counterfeit because Satan is "not permitted to counterfeit the manner of Christ's advent" (*The Great Controversy*, "The Time of Trouble"). When the real Christ appears, it will be in the clouds with power and great glory, accompanied by millions of angels, and His true followers will recognize His voice (*The Great Controversy*, "The Time of Trouble").
