## Entry `ilk-021a`

- `question`: `Is there any biblical prophetic connection between Israel's 400 years of bondage and the black slavery experience, especially in the Americas?`
- `scripture_basis`: `Philemon; no direct biblical prophecy is cited connecting the two histories`
- `secondary_support`: `Ellen White on racism, American slavery, and the Civil War`
- `tags`: `slavery, race, black-history, Egypt, Philemon, Civil-War, racism, Adventist-history`

### Answer

No direct biblical prophecy is given here that ties Israel's 400 years of bondage to the black slavery experience in a one-to-one prophetic way.

Do not make that connection dogmatically. You can find broad parallels in the Bible about oppression, injustice, and God's concern for the downtrodden, but not a specific prophecy saying that the black slavery experience in the Americas fulfills Israel's bondage in Egypt.

Philemon is used to show another important distinction. Slavery in the ancient world was not identical to the dehumanizing, race-based system of American slavery. Paul sends Onesimus back, but he tells Philemon to receive him no longer merely as a servant, but as a beloved brother. That is a very different moral atmosphere from the cruelty and racial degradation that marked modern chattel slavery.

God opposed the high crime of slavery and overruled history against it. But do not conclude from that that the black experience is a direct prophetic duplicate of Israel's 400 years. The safer conclusion is that racism is one more expression of the evil heart of man, that God sees it, and that His people should reject it.

## Entry `ilk-021b`

- `question`: `Can you build a biblical study on the idea of 6000 years of sin, or is that mainly a Spirit of Prophecy concept?`
- `scripture_basis`: `Psalm 90:4; 2 Peter 3:8; Revelation 20:2-7`
- `secondary_support`: `Ellen White references on 6000 years; The Great Controversy, p. 659`
- `tags`: `6000-years, chronology, millennium, Psalm-90, 2-Peter-3, Revelation-20, date-setting`

### Answer

You should not build an exact end-time date chart on the `6000 years of sin` idea.

Scripture does give you thousand-year language. Psalm 90 says that a thousand years in God's sight are like a day, 2 Peter 3 repeats that principle, and Revelation 20 gives the explicit thousand years of the millennium. So the Bible does contain the broader categories that make the idea understandable.

But the specific `6000 years` formulation should be taken more as a general Spirit of Prophecy concept than as a precise biblical countdown that lets you calculate the exact end.

So do not use it to peg a date for Christ's return. The point is not that you have been given a secret stopwatch. The point is that human history is far advanced, the time is nearly exhausted, and you should live in readiness rather than in date-setting speculation.
