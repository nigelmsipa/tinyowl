## Entry `ilk-001a`

- `question`: `How does the 2300-day prophecy lead to 1844 if some argue that Scripture never teaches a day-for-a-year principle?`
- `scripture_basis`: `Ezekiel 4:6; Numbers 14:34; Daniel 7:25; Daniel 8:14; Daniel 9:24-27`
- `tags`: `prophecy, 2300-days, 1844, Daniel, year-day-principle, chronology`

### Answer

The 2300-day prophecy reaches 1844 by applying the day-for-a-year principle to symbolic prophecy. Ezekiel 4:6 and Numbers 14:34 establish the biblical principle of a symbolic day standing for a year. Daniel 7:25 then shows symbolic prophetic time inside a chapter filled with symbols, so its time expression is understood symbolically as well.

The same principle also applies to the seventy weeks of Daniel 9, which are understood as years. From there, Daniel 9:25 supplies the starting point in 457 BC, the command to restore and build Jerusalem. On that basis, the 2300 days of Daniel 8:14 are understood as prophetic years extending to the autumn of 1844.

## Entry `ilk-001b`

- `question`: `If the 2300-year prophecy did not arrive at 1844, would the Seventh-day Adventist movement collapse?`
- `scripture_basis`: `Daniel 8:14; Revelation 10`
- `tags`: `1844, sanctuary, Adventist-movement, remnant, doctrine`

### Answer

No. The movement does not rest on an invented idea, but on truths already present in Scripture and rediscovered through study. At the same time, 1844 is foundational because Daniel 8:14 locates Christ's work in the heavenly sanctuary in a decisive end-time phase.

Revelation 10 also helps explain the rise of the Advent movement. From that sanctuary-centered framework, 1844 connects to judgment, the continuing authority of God's law, the Sabbath, the state of the dead, healthful living, and the identity of the remnant church. So the movement does not stand on a human invention, but on a larger scriptural structure in which 1844 is a crucial prophetic foundation.

## Entry `ilk-001c`

- `question`: `How do you count from BC to AD when working through this prophecy?`
- `scripture_basis`: `Daniel 9:25`
- `tags`: `chronology, BC-AD, prophecy, 1844`

### Answer

Count forward from the starting point tied to Daniel 9:25 without inserting a year zero. The chronology moves from 1 BC directly to AD 1, so the number one is counted only once. If you count as though there were a year zero, the prophetic calculation comes out wrong.
