# I'd Like to Know — Canonical Q&A Review

## 001. Who Will Be Sealed?

**Q:** What does “present truth” or “truth for this time” mean?

Present truth is truth tied to what Christ is doing right now — specifically, His high-priestly work in the Most Holy Place of the heavenly sanctuary since 1844.

**Q:** Will both immature and mature believers be sealed before probation closes?

Yes — and the answer turns on what sealing actually requires. The seal is not a grade of spiritual maturity; it is a mark of ownership and allegiance.

## 002. Do We Share in Others' Sins? + Coals of Fire Explained

**Q:** What does 1 Timothy 5:22 mean about not sharing in the sins of others?

1 Timothy 5:22 sits inside Paul's sustained instructions to Timothy on managing elders. From verse 17 onward, Paul addresses how elders are honored, how accusations against them are handled, and how they are to be rebuked when guilty. Verse 21 commands Timothy to observe these things "without prejudice, doing nothing by partiality." Then verse 22: "Do not lay hands on anyone hastily, nor share in other people's sins. Keep yourself pure."

**Q:** What does it mean to heap coals of fire on an enemy's head in Proverbs 25:21-22?

Proverbs 25:21-22 establishes the instruction before naming its effect: "If your enemy is hungry, give him bread to eat; and if he is thirsty, give him water to drink: for so you will heap coals of fire upon his head, and the Lord will reward you." The sequence matters — give first, coals follow. The coals are not the motive; they are the consequence of genuine, unguarded kindness.

## 003. The Mark of the Beast –Microchips & Biometric Systems

**Q:** What is the mark of the beast according to the Bible, and is it a microchip or biometric system?

The mark of the beast is not a microchip, barcode, biometric scan, or some other tracking device. Those things may become mechanisms of control, but they are not the mark itself.

## 004. Is Deception Ever Justified?

**Q:** Where in the Bible does it say the apostolic church had practices like salutes, drills, uniforms, and marching such as Pathfinders and AY clubs do?

It does not. The apostolic church does not show salutes, drills, uniforms, or marching — and it also does not show cooking demonstrations, health lectures, or medical cadet corps. Absence in the New Testament is not by itself a prohibition. As long as a practice does not contradict what the New Testament church did and is ordered toward the church's mission, the church is free to adopt it.

**Q:** Based on 1 Kings 20:35-43, is it okay to use a false name and pretend to be someone else so that an elder who has stopped responding to emails will respond?

No. Tell the truth. Leave the consequences with God.

## 005. How to Start Keeping the Sabbath + Tithing Questions Answered

**Q:** How should someone transition to keeping the Sabbath after discovering the truth, without simply turning his back on his former church?

If you have become convinced from Scripture that the Sabbath is truth, then honor that truth fully. Do not try to keep one foot in obedience and one foot in a church system that rejects the light God has shown you.

**Q:** How should Christians practice tithing today, especially with fixed salaries, gross-versus-net questions, financial strain, and concerns about how leaders use the money?

Treat tithing as a faith issue before you treat it as an accounting puzzle.

## 006. Are Children Born Sinful? + Trumpets & Crocheting on Sabbath – is it work?

**Q:** Does 1 Corinthians 7:14 — that the children of a mixed marriage "would be unclean, but now they are holy" — mean children are born with a sinful nature?

No, this is not a text about a child's nature at birth. It is about the home.

**Q:** How does the seventh commandment apply to angels and other non-human beings who do not marry? And why do animals procreate if they are not repopulating heaven?

The ten commandments are eternal in principle and adapted in expression. The fourth commandment, for example, mentions the manservant and maidservant — that is a human application of an eternal principle. The seventh commandment works the same way.

**Q:** Can you show from the Bible only that the saints will hear the day and hour of Christ's coming?

There is no single Bible verse that states, "God will announce the day and the hour." The speakers acknowledge that directly. What the Bible does give is the surrounding scene, and the announcement fits inside it.

**Q:** Is it wrong to crochet on Sabbath while listening to Bibles or sermons to keep my hands busy, if it is not to sell?

No moral issue here. Do not build lists of Sabbath rules that Scripture does not build. The biblical principles are plain — do not buy or sell, do not engage in secular work — and inside those principles God has given you a conscience. A sanctified conscience is the instrument that leads you to the right answer in your own case.

## 007. The Torn Veil & Christ’s Two-Phase Ministry Explained

**Q:** Why did the veil separating the holy and most holy place in the heavenly sanctuary continue to exist until 1844? How do we reconcile the rending of the earthly veil at the cross with Christ's two-phase priestly ministry in heaven?

The rending of the veil at Christ's death signified the end of the earthly sanctuary system, not the abolition of the heavenly sanctuary or its phases. Hebrews 8 says the earthly tabernacle was only a type and shadow of the true heavenly reality. When Jesus died, the veil of His flesh was torn, putting an end to the validity of earthly animal sacrifices in the sense explained by Daniel 9 and opening direct access to God through Christ.

**Q:** Who served as the high priest in the heavenly sanctuary before Jesus's inauguration as our high priest at His ascension?

Strictly speaking, no one served as a fully inaugurated high priest before Christ, because the blood of bulls and goats could not actually remove sin. In the Old Testament, God passed over sins on credit, as Romans 3:25 explains. Salvation was always through faith in the coming Savior, whose future sacrifice guaranteed their pardon.

## 008. Israel's Lost Tribes + Why God Allowed War

**Q:** If the ten northern tribes lost their pure lineage in the Assyrian captivity, how could Elizabeth be identified as from the tribe of Levi and Anna as from the tribe of Asher?

Not every Israelite in the northern tribes lost their identity. A good portion intermarried with the Assyrians and the surrounding pagan nations — that is the origin of the Samaritans — but a remnant in each tribe remained faithful and kept the line. Josiah's reformation shows it clearly: when he reached into the territory of the northern tribes, there were people there who returned to the Lord.

**Q:** How can God sanction one nation attacking and killing another, as with Israel and the Moabites in 2 Kings 3?

God does not destroy a nation arbitrarily. He destroys it when it has gone beyond the point of no return, and only He knows when that line is crossed.

**Q:** If wars seem fewer now than in Old Testament times, what did Jesus mean in Matthew 24 about wars and rumors of wars as a sign of the end?

The sign is not that there are wars for the first time. Wars have always existed. What Jesus is describing is a convergence — wars on a worldwide scale, more bloody, more repetitive, combined with all the other signs.

**Q:** Why did Ellen White call the 1888 message "the third angel's message in verity," and if it had been accepted at Minneapolis would it have brought the latter rain and finished the work?

Yes. Had the message been received, it would have brought the latter rain and finished the work.

## 009. Is Self-Defense Sin? + Revelation 17:12 a Literal Time Prophecy?

**Q:** What is the meaning of Revelation 9?

Revelation 9 should be read as part of the historicist unfolding of the trumpets, not as a chapter pushed wholesale into the future. The chapter contains the fifth and sixth trumpets, and the speakers insist that the trumpets, like the churches and the seals, move through history step by step from John's time to the end.

**Q:** Is the "one hour" in Revelation 17:12 a literal time prophecy?

No. The "one hour" in Revelation 17:12 is treated here as a short but indefinite period, not as a literal prophetic time calculation.

**Q:** Does the sixth commandment forbid self-defense?

The sixth commandment forbids willful, violent, intentional taking of life. The speakers do not flatten every case of death into the same category. They say Scripture itself distinguishes between premeditated murder, accidental killing, vengeance, and cases where a death happens without lying in wait.

## 010. The Holy Spirit, Memory in Heaven & the Filioque Debate

**Q:** Why is the Holy Spirit sometimes referred to as "he" and other times as "it" in Scripture, as in Romans 8:26-27 and John 14-16?

The variation between "he" and "it" is a grammar issue, not a theology issue. In Greek, the word for spirit — pneuma — is grammatically neuter, and the article that goes with it is the neuter article. A literal translation can render that as "it." But when the context makes plain that the subject is the Holy Spirit and the Holy Spirit is a person, translators shift to "he" to reflect the referent rather than the grammatical gender. That is why the same translator can use both within a few verses. Nothing in the Greek requires reading the Holy Spirit as an impersonal force.

**Q:** If heaven has no sin or sadness, how will we remember loved ones who did not make it?

The premise needs one correction before the answer. It is not quite right to say heaven has never had sin or sadness. Sin originated in heaven, in a perfect place. And sadness is not sin — the angels stopped their singing and laid down their harps during Christ's passion, and God Himself sorrows when people reject Him. What is true is that heaven now has no sin, because the devil has been cast out, and in the new earth there will be no more pain.

**Q:** Is the Filioque how they supplant Jesus Christ with the pope?

The premise behind the question does not hold up, and the speakers say so directly. They do not see how the Filioque is connected to supplanting Christ with the pope.

## 011. Should Christians follow the Gregorian calendar?

**Q:** Should Christians follow the Gregorian calendar?

Using the Gregorian calendar for daily life and business is perfectly acceptable and does not violate biblical principles. The calendar is a civil framework, an ordinance of men that Christians can freely navigate to maintain order in society, which is the principle drawn from 1 Peter 2.

**Q:** What is the SDA Church’s position on White Christian Nationalism?

The Seventh-day Adventist Church is completely against Christian nationalism. Any ideology that attempts to merge church and state to create a "Christian nation" or use political authority to enforce religion is entirely antithetical to Adventist theology and core values.

## 012. Armageddon – Before or After the Millennium?

**Q:** Does Jesus see what is happening on earth directly, or through the Holy Spirit? Is He only relying on foreknowledge, and how does this relate to the Godhead and the ministry of angels?

Christ does not need to leave heaven and become locally present on earth in order to know what is happening here. God knows everything from eternity, and Christ shares that divine knowledge. At the same time, heaven does not operate in a flat, undifferentiated way. God works through an ordered administration.

**Q:** Does Armageddon happen before or after the Millennium?

Armageddon, strictly speaking, takes place before the second coming, not after the millennium.

## 013. Is It Wrong to Show Affection in Church? How Should We Behave in Church?

**Q:** Is it wrong to show physical affection — stroking, holding — during church service, and how should people conduct themselves in the house of God?

The purpose of the worship service is to focus on God — to pray, sing, hear the Word, and direct attention upward. Anything that pulls that focus away, whether from the person doing it or those sitting nearby, is out of place. 1 Corinthians 14:40 is the standard: "Let all things be done decently and in order."

**Q:** In Revelation 5:6, Jesus is described as having seven eyes which are the seven spirits of God. Does this mean Jesus sees what is happening on earth through the Holy Spirit? And is the Holy Spirit a person or a force or energy coming from the Father and the Son?

On personhood there is no ambiguity. Ellen White names three living persons of the heavenly trio — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — in Bible Training School, March 1, 1906. The Holy Spirit has intelligence, can be grieved, and is volitional; John 3:8 says the Spirit blows where he wills. He convicts, sanctifies, gives gifts. He is a person.

## 014. Why did God allow slavery in Israel — especially of foreigners?

**Q:** Is "Immanuel" in Isaiah 7:14 really about Jesus, and how do we explain the time-bound prophecy of verse 16?

This prophecy must be understood through biblical typology, where a historical "type" (a shadow or pattern) points to a future "antitype" (the perfect, ultimate reality).

**Q:** How do we interpret the institution of slavery in ancient Israel, especially the lifelong servitude of non-Israelites?

Biblical slavery cannot be equated with Greco-Roman slavery or modern colonial slavery. Those later systems treated human beings as mere property to be exploited with "rigor"—the exact kind of abusive bondage Israel suffered in Egypt, which God condemned. In fact, God punished the United States for the high crime of slavery.

## 015. What Happens When We Die? + Is Ezekiel 9 for the End Times?

**Q:** What is the difference between nephesh in Genesis 2:7 and ruach in Ecclesiastes 12:7, and if they are different words why do we still say that no conscious part of a person goes to God at death?

`Nephesh` and `ruach` are not the same word, but they are not describing two conscious parts of a human being either.

Ezekiel 9 had a real historical fulfillment, and it also has a typological end-time application. You do not have to choose one and deny the other.

## 016. Understanding Daniel & Revelation + What Did Jesus Mean in John 20:17?

**Q:** How can I understand the prophecies of Daniel 1–11 and the book of Revelation?

To understand the prophetic books, you must study them in sequence and recognize their deep interconnectedness. Revelation is the key that unlocks the culmination of Daniel, but Daniel lays the foundation. You cannot study the complexities of Revelation without first mastering Daniel, just as you cannot study trigonometry without first studying basic math.

**Q:** What did Jesus mean when He said “Touch Me Not” in John 20:17?

When Jesus told Mary "touch me not," a better translation from the Greek is "do not cling to me" or "do not detain me." Jesus was not forbidding physical contact entirely—He allowed it later—but He could not be delayed at that specific moment. He had to make a quick ascension to the heavenly courts to present Himself before the Father.

## 017. Still Called a Sinner? Synagogue of Satan Explained

**Q:** When a sinner comes to repentance and turns their life around, is that person still labeled a sinner?

Technically yes and no — and that tension is the heart of justification by faith. Romans 5:1 is the ground: "Having been justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ." Justification means God looks at the repentant, believing sinner and declares them righteous — not because sin has ceased, but because Christ's righteousness is credited to their account. Romans 8:1 says it from the other direction: "There is therefore now no condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus."

**Q:** Who are the people who claim to be Jews and are not, and what is the synagogue of Satan in Revelation 2:9?

The phrase appears in exactly two places: Revelation 2:9, the letter to Smyrna, and Revelation 3:9, the letter to Philadelphia. Both say the same thing — "those who say they are Jews and are not, but are a synagogue of Satan." Reading this as a reference to ethnic Jewish people is to misread the book of Revelation, which is saturated with Old Testament Jewish terminology applied to the church age: Balaam, Jezebel, the synagogue itself — all Old Testament names and institutions applied to conditions within the Christian church.

## 018. Tithing Struggles in Marriage + What Does Revelation 17:10 Mean?

**Q:** I am an Adventist who wants to tithe, but my non-Christian husband forbids it and controls our household income. The Bible says to submit to a husband, but also to obey God rather than man. Am I accountable for not tithing? Am I at risk of losing salvation over this?

Submission to a husband does not extend to disobeying God. Ephesians 5:22 says "wives submit yourselves unto your own husbands as unto the Lord" — the qualifier matters. The submission is modeled on the wife's submission to the Lord, which means it cannot require what the Lord himself forbids. There is a ceiling on spousal authority, and "obey God rather than man" is that ceiling.

**Q:** In Revelation 17:10, five heads have fallen, one is, and the other has not yet come. If the fifth head is papal Rome and John's vision was written before papal Rome, how is John viewing this after the fifth head has already fallen?

The answer is in the vantage point of the vision. Revelation 17:1-2 opens with one of the seven bowl angels saying to John, "Come, I will show you the judgment of the great harlot who sits on many waters." John is not viewing this from his own time in the first century. He is being transported to the end time — to the period when the papacy has recovered from its deadly wound and the multitudes, nations, and tongues are once again supporting her.

## 021. Buying & Selling in Revelation – Literal or Symbolic?

**Q:** What is your position on deliverance ministry? Is there a rightful place for this within the church?

Deliverance ministry has a legitimate place in the church, but the term requires careful definition. In its broadest sense all ministry is deliverance ministry: when a person surrenders to Christ, Romans 6:14-16 declares that sin no longer has dominion, and 2 Corinthians 5:17 confirms that former things pass away — the power of sin is broken. This progressive freedom from sin's power, and eventually its presence, is the biblical shape of deliverance.

**Q:** What does buying and selling mean in Revelation 13:17 — is it literal or spiritual?

Revelation 13:16-17 states plainly that no one may buy or sell except those who bear the mark of the beast. The restriction is literal: commerce will be closed to those who refuse the mark. But the question of why — the spiritual condition that triggers the literal consequence — is what Proverbs 23:23 unlocks.

**Q:** Who is the woman clothed with the sun in Revelation 12, and what do the moon and the 12 stars on her head represent?

In Revelation 12:1-2 a woman appears clothed with the sun, the moon under her feet, and a crown of twelve stars — she is in labor. The woman in Revelation represents the church. A pregnant church births only one thing: Christ.

The identification of Daniel 8's little horn as Antiochus Epiphanes fails on theological, chronological, and exegetical grounds.

## 023. Did the Judgment Begin in 1844?

**Q:** As Adventists we believe the judgment began in 1844. Others — evangelicals and some former Adventists such as Desmond Ford — believe the judgment begins right after the resurrection of Christ in 31 AD. Can you reconcile these two ideas with biblical text? Which one is true?

A trial has three stages: investigation of the evidence, verdict or sentence, and execution of the sentence. God’s judgment follows that biblical pattern. He does not simply declare someone saved or lost; there is a universe to convince that God has been right in every case, and that must happen before the second coming.

**Q:** Concerning Revelation 1:7 — “every eye will see him, even those that pierced him.” We believe those who crucified Christ will be resurrected to see his coming. When they receive the second death, will it actually be their third death? This verse says “every eye will see him” — will the righteous dead see Jesus approaching with the clouds, or only once he arrives and is in the clouds?

The Bible does not speak of a third death. It speaks of first death — physical death, which everyone will suffer except the end‑time generation — and second death, which is everlasting death. Physical death is not eternal. In the case of the second coming, several groups must be kept in mind. Those who are alive will see Jesus come in the clouds. There is also a specialized group of dead — those who pierced him — who will resurrect. And there is a group of righteous people — those who died in the faith of the third angel’s message — who resurrect at the voice of God before Jesus even begins his second coming. Then when Jesus is above the earth, the other righteous dead resurrect. They are not going to see the entire second coming from beginning to end.

## 024. Did God Allow "Strong Drink"? Deuteronomy 14:26 Explained

**Q:** Deuteronomy 14:26 says to spend the tithe money on "wine or similar drink" — was strong drink actually permitted by God in Old Testament times? How do we understand this in light of the whole Bible?

Deuteronomy 14:26 is not a license to drink intoxicants. Read in context, it is part of the second-tithe instruction for pilgrimage feasts — a provision for long-distance travel where the tithe in kind could not be transported. The text says "whatever your heart desires" because it is describing how to convert grain and livestock into something portable. It is not issuing a blanket endorsement of any substance that produces the heart's desire.
